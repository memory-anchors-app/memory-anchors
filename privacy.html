Privacy Policy

Anchor Lab · Last updated: 12 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Anchor Lab app ("the App", "we", "us") handles your information. We designed the App to keep your data on your device whenever possible and to collect as little as we can.

1. Data stored on your device

Your study material — labs, cards, anchors, and your learning progress — is stored locally on your device using Apple's on-device storage. We do not hold a copy of this content on our servers, except for items you explicitly choose to share (see "Content you choose to share" below).

2. Content you choose to share

The App lets you create a shareable link for a card or a lab. This feature is optional and only runs when you tap Share.

  • When you share, the selected content — its text and images — is uploaded to our servers so the link can be opened. Images are uploaded as compressed copies. Only the card or lab you share is uploaded; your other content stays on your device.
  • Anyone who has the link can view the shared content. Links are unguessable but not password-protected, so treat a share link like any unlisted URL and only send it to people you trust.
  • Shared content is automatically deleted from our servers after 30 days.
  • By sharing, you grant us permission to host and transmit that content so the link works. You are responsible for what you share: you confirm you have the right to share it and agree not to share unlawful, infringing, or otherwise objectionable material. We may remove shared content at any time.

3. YouTube video playback

The App uses YouTube API Services to let you embed and play YouTube videos as part of your study material. By using this feature, you are also agreeing to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's use of information collected via YouTube API Services is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

4. AI generation

When you use AI features (generating or refining cards, AI chat, or web-grounded answers), the text you provide — including prompts and any source material you add, such as YouTube transcripts and timing data — is sent to a third-party AI provider solely to produce a response for you. This provider may process the content on its servers. We do not use this content for advertising and do not sell it. We currently use Google (Gemini); please review the Gemini API and Google Privacy Policy.

5. Subscriptions and payments

Subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive or store your payment card details. Apple shares with us only non-identifying purchase status needed to unlock paid features. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

6. Analytics and crash logs

To improve the App, we may collect anonymous usage analytics (for example, onboarding steps completed) and diagnostic crash logs. This information is not used to identify you personally.

7. Account and email (if provided)

The App may, in the future, let you create an account or contact us using your email address. If you provide an email, we use it only to operate the account and to respond to you. We do not sell your email address.

8. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

9. Your choices

You can delete your locally stored content at any time from within the App or by removing the App. You can manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple ID settings.

10. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
